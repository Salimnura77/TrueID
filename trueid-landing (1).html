The problem

Nigeria verifies the same person, over and over.

Every bank, lender, and service provider repeats the same identity checks — costing time, money, and trust.

Multiple ID systems

NIN, BVN, Passport, and Voter's Card each live in a different silo, with no single source of truth.

Repeated KYC fatigue

Users re-submit the same documents to every new bank, lender, or platform they sign up with.

Fraud & forgery

Manually reviewed paper documents remain easy to counterfeit and expensive to verify at scale.

Financial exclusion

Rural and unbanked Nigerians are locked out of digital services they can't easily verify for.

Slow onboarding

Account opening can take days when identity has to be manually cross-checked at every step.

Privacy risk

Sensitive documents get copied and stored across dozens of separate, inconsistently secured systems.
